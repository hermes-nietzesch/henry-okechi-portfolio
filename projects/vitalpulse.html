Clinical & MedTech · Interactive Prototype

VitalPulse.

An emergency blood-donation response prototype designed to turn urgent clinical requests into fast, frictionless donor mobilization.

Visual showcase for VitalPulse's emergency donor-alert interface.

The Challenge

In acute medical emergencies, sourcing matched blood donors often collapses into panicked social broadcasts with missing urgency context and high donor drop-off.

What I Built & Directed

VitalPulse models a calm, high-clarity mobile donor network: presenting urgency-ranked emergency blood alerts, one-tap donor acceptance flows, donor eligibility education, and lives-impacted telemetry.

Key Proof & Outcomes

  • Applies frontline medical understanding of acute blood-shortage bottlenecks directly into mobile product UX.
  • Complete donor journey architecture built in React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
  • Available for live walkthrough during private healthtech interviews (private repository).

Core Features & Architecture

  • Urgency-tiered emergency donor-request feed with blood-type & proximity framing
  • Streamlined alert acceptance and confirmation demo workflow
  • Donor profile management, clinical eligibility learning, and personal impact metrics
  • Mobile-first responsive navigation and purposeful clinical micro-interactions

04 — MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY / EDUCATION

MedFlash (MCQ Recall).

A physician-built board review application that converts raw multiple-choice question PDFs into structured active-recall study sessions.

ACTUAL DEPLOYED CAPTURE · LIVE — Live dark-mode board review workspace at mcqrecall.netlify.app.

Overview

Born directly from medical exam preparation, MedFlash (MCQ Recall) turns uploaded multi-file MCQ PDFs into focused active-recall study sessions—paired with a built-in Pomodoro timer, session history, and a low-fatigue dark interface.

The Problem

Medical students and doctors preparing for board exams routinely waste hours reformatting past-question PDFs instead of practicing active recall under timed conditions.

Product Concept

Remove setup friction completely: drop one or more MCQ PDFs into the browser, treat each document as a resumable study session, and pair active recall with an integrated Pomodoro workflow.

Approach & Implementation

Built a zero-signup client-side web workflow deployed to Netlify, featuring multi-file PDF drag-and-drop ingestion (supporting 'Correct Answer X' formatting) and four focused navigation views: Sessions, Pomodoro, History, and Settings.

Key Features

  • Multi-file drag-and-drop PDF uploader with automatic MCQ and answer-key parsing
  • Session-based workspace turning every uploaded PDF into a resumable study deck
  • Built-in Pomodoro focus timer for high-volume medical revision blocks
  • Persistent study history and customizable review settings in a distraction-free dark UI

Challenges & Decisions

Eliminating account walls and supporting multi-file PDF drops directly on the landing screen ensured students can move from raw past questions to active recall in seconds.

Outcome & Current Status

Live and publicly accessible at mcqrecall.netlify.app.

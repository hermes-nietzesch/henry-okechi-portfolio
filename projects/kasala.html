05 — GAME DEVELOPMENT / MULTIPLAYER

Kasala.

A ten-player browser battle royale and 5v5 arena shooter set in Computer Village, Lagos—built with an AI-assisted 3D asset pipeline and deployed on Cloudflare Workers.

ACTUAL DEPLOYED CAPTURE · PLAYABLE — Live lobby capture from kasala.kasala.workers.dev displaying four playable 3D Lagos character classes and match modes.

Overview

Kasala brings compact multiplayer 3D action directly to the browser without app-store downloads. Set in Computer Village, Lagos, it supports Solo and Team Battle Royale, 5v5 Team Battle, Free-for-All, configurable AI bots, and a Gun Range.

The Problem

3D multiplayer shooters are traditionally locked behind heavy native installs and large studio pipelines, leaving culturally grounded West African settings rarely represented in instant web games.

Product Concept

Pair an AI-assisted 3D character and audio production workflow with lightweight browser delivery on Cloudflare Workers so players can launch matches in one click.

Approach & Implementation

Directed character creation across four recognizable Lagos archetypes, structured multiple match modes (Battle Royale, 5v5, Free-for-All, Bots, Gun Range), and shipped globally on Cloudflare Workers.

Key Features

  • Ten-player online Battle Royale (Solo & Teams) plus 5v5 Team Battle and Free-for-All
  • Online matchmaking, private friend lobbies, Bot difficulty levels (Easy / Medium / Hard), and Gun Range
  • Four custom AI-assisted 3D character renders and full audio/music direction
  • Zero-install browser play delivered via Cloudflare Workers

Challenges & Decisions

Including offline-friendly bot tiers and a dedicated Gun Range alongside online lobbies ensures solo visitors can experience gameplay immediately even outside peak lobby hours.

Outcome & Current Status

Live and playable at kasala.kasala.workers.dev.
